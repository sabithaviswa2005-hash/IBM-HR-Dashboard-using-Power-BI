# IBM HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Power BI to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, employee engagement, and career growth trends.

The dashboard helps HR professionals and business leaders identify workforce challenges, understand attrition drivers, and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention.

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition is a critical challenge that affects productivity, operational continuity, and organizational performance.

The objective of this project is to:

- Analyze workforce demographics
- Identify employee attrition patterns
- Understand factors influencing employee turnover
- Evaluate employee satisfaction and engagement
- Assess career growth and promotion trends
- Provide actionable HR recommendations

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary

Provides a high-level overview of the workforce.

KPIs:

- Total Employees
- Active Employees
- Attrition Count
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income

Business Questions:

- What is the current workforce size?
- What is the overall attrition rate?
- Which departments contribute most to attrition?

---

### 2. Attrition Analysis

Identifies employee groups most affected by turnover.

Analysis Includes:

- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Income Group
- Attrition by Business Travel
- Attrition by Marital Status
- Attrition by Education Field

Key Insight:

Employees aged 26–35 and Sales-related roles experience the highest attrition.

---

### 3. Satisfaction & Engagement

Evaluates the relationship between employee engagement and turnover.

Analysis Includes:

- Job Satisfaction
- Environment Satisfaction
- Work-Life Balance
- Job Involvement
- Satisfaction Heat Map
- Income vs Satisfaction Analysis

Key Insight:

Lower satisfaction levels are associated with higher attrition.

---

### 4. Career Growth & Retention

Analyzes career progression and employee retention.

Analysis Includes:

- Attrition by Tenure Group
- Attrition by Job Level
- Promotion Gap Analysis
- Experience Analysis
- Income Growth Analysis

Key Insight:

Employees with limited career growth opportunities show higher turnover tendencies.

---

### 5. AI Insights & Recommendations

Uses Power BI AI features to identify key attrition drivers.

Features:

- Key Influencers
- Risk Matrix
- Attrition Driver Analysis
- Strategic Recommendations

Key Insight:

Overtime, Job Satisfaction, Monthly Income, and Job Role are major factors influencing attrition.

---

## Key Findings

- Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Research & Development department records the highest attrition volume.
- Sales-related roles show elevated attrition risk.
- Employees aged 26–35 represent the largest attrition segment.
- Overtime is strongly associated with employee turnover.
- Job Satisfaction and Work-Life Balance significantly impact retention.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Reduce excessive overtime through workforce planning.
2. Strengthen retention strategies for high-risk job roles.
3. Improve employee engagement programs.
4. Enhance career development and promotion opportunities.
5. Monitor attrition trends through periodic workforce reviews.
